# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Directory and discovery pages
- Winery profile pages
- Blog listing and blog post pages
- Public utility pages such as the map and about page

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboards
- Admin pages
- Internal API routes except where they affect public crawlability

## Target audience
- Dog owners planning winery visits in Napa Valley and Sonoma County
- Travelers looking for dog-friendly wineries, tasting rooms, and wine country guides

## Primary keywords
- Dog-friendly wineries Napa Valley
- Dog-friendly wineries Sonoma County
- Napa Valley wineries dogs allowed
- Sonoma dog-friendly wineries
- Dog-friendly wine country guide

- None yet
